# Portfolio Content Plan

## 1. Messaging and tone

The portfolio serves non-technical business owners first, while remaining credible to recruiters and engineers.

Positioning:

**Full-stack developer building websites, web applications, APIs and AI-enabled integrations.**

Draft supporting statement:

“I help businesses turn ideas and operational challenges into practical digital solutions — from professional websites and custom web applications to backend systems, API integrations and AI-powered features.”

Use plain, confident language:
- Explain the problem before listing technologies.
- Describe what the product enables.
- Make the developer's role explicit.
- Avoid unexplained acronyms and exaggerated claims.
- Do not overstate business results or project maturity.
- Make technical depth available without forcing non-technical visitors to read it.

## 2. Brand presentation

- The portfolio is under the developer's personal name.
- Use the approved professional avatar consistently in the hero and About section.
- Abeeb Digital Studio appears as a separate business founded by the developer. Keep it secondary in the About section or footer.
- Do not present the portfolio as an Abeeb Digital Studio agency website.
- Keep the voice direct, helpful and collaborative.

## 3. Homepage narrative

### Header
Personal-name wordmark; Work, Services, Process and About navigation; primary “Book a Discovery Call” action.

### Hero
Must answer:
1. Who is this?
2. What kind of problems can they solve?
3. What should the visitor do next?

Draft eyebrow:
“FULL-STACK DEVELOPER · WEB APPLICATIONS · AI INTEGRATION”

Draft headline:
“I turn business ideas into useful digital products.”

Draft supporting copy:
“I design and build fast, reliable websites and web applications, connect the systems businesses depend on, and add AI where it creates real value.”

Primary CTA: “Book a Discovery Call”
Secondary CTA: “Explore My Work”

Use the professional avatar as the human focal point, with a restrained 3D visual as supporting decoration. Do not let visual effects delay or obscure the message.

### Capability summary
Introduce the four core service areas:
- Website development.
- Web application development.
- Backend and API development.
- AI integration.

### Selected work
Lead with projects that demonstrate breadth and ownership:
- **Ritewheel Autos** — automotive marketplace / discovery product.
- **FixTrust** — artisan and property-project marketplace.

Each card should explain the product in one sentence and show a real product image. Link to a case study and only to a live product when the current destination and functionality have been checked.

### Services
Describe each service in terms of what a business can achieve. Explain tools and technologies in supporting detail rather than leading with a long list of frameworks.

### Process
Describe a reassuring delivery flow:
1. Discover the business problem and users.
2. Define scope, priorities and constraints.
3. Build in visible increments.
4. Test and refine.
5. Launch and hand over.

Do not promise delivery timelines before understanding the scope.

### About
Explain the developer's full-stack focus, product-building approach and interest in solving practical business problems. Keep the biography concise and factual. Include:
- Professional avatar.
- Short introduction.
- Link to downloadable CV.
- A discreet note that the developer founded Abeeb Digital Studio.

Do not imply employment or commercial client engagements that did not occur.

### Contact and conversion
Use a direct invitation such as:

“Have an idea you want to bring to life?”

Explain that the first conversation is a chance to understand the business and work out what should be built.

Primary CTA: **Book a Discovery Call** — opens the selected scheduler.
Secondary CTA: **Chat on WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled introduction.

The booking provider and WhatsApp number must be configured and tested before launch. An optional short enquiry form may remain available, but do not force a long questionnaire.

### Footer
Include personal name/wordmark, concise role statement, key navigation, approved professional links, CV link and booking/WhatsApp contact actions. Abeeb Digital Studio remains secondary.

## 4. Case-study template

Every case study should follow a consistent structure:

1. Overview — what the product is and who it serves.
2. Problem — the user or business need.
3. Product approach — the solution and user journey.
4. My contribution — what was personally designed, implemented, integrated or maintained.
5. Features — only verified functionality, grouped by status if needed.
6. Architecture — high-level technical explanation.
7. Challenges and trade-offs — real engineering decisions.
8. Current status — deployed, prototype, in progress or another accurate label.
9. Links — live demo and public source when appropriate.

Use real screenshots and concise captions. Give non-technical readers the product story first; put deeper technical details later in the case study.

## 5. Ritewheel Autos content checklist

Verify before publishing:
- Exact product scope and intended audience.
- Which customer flows work in production.
- Which pages or features are demonstrable.
- AI concierge capabilities and limitations.
- Database and deployment architecture.
- Any known issues or demo-data caveats.
- Current screenshots captured from the deployed product.
- Correct live URL and repository link, if public.

Do not describe demo listings as real dealership inventory unless confirmed.

## 6. FixTrust content checklist

Verify before publishing:
- Product scope and intended users.
- Current operational and deployment status.
- Which verification and escrow flows are actually working.
- Which payment integrations are live versus test or incomplete.
- Which marketplace and project-management features are demonstrable.
- Infrastructure and database decisions.
- Important engineering challenges and lessons.
- Current screenshots, live URL and public source where appropriate.

Clearly label unfinished integrations and avoid implying real escrow funds are being held if that is not true.

## 7. CV

Include:
- Professional summary consistent with the portfolio positioning.
- Skills grounded in real work.
- Project experience with links.
- Education and learning history as factually presented.
- Contact information approved for public distribution.

The downloadable PDF must be current, readable, ATS-friendly and aligned with the site's positioning.

## 8. Evidence required before publication

- Approved avatar asset.
- Current project screenshots.
- Live URLs verified.
- Public repository links checked.
- Correct project status and feature claims.
- Current CV PDF.
- Approved public name, email and social links.
- Booking provider URL and configured availability.
- Approved WhatsApp contact number.
- Any testimonials or metrics independently verified before use.
